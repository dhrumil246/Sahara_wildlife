Report elephants you see

If elephants are near

  • Stay indoors. Keep children and livestock inside.
  • Keep at least 100 metres away. Give them a clear path back to the forest.
  • Move in groups after dark, with a torch.
  • Call the forest team and wait for them.
  • Do not go near them, chase them or take photos close up.
  • Do not throw stones or burst crackers at them. It makes them angry.
  • Do not keep stored grain or liquor in the house when elephants are around.
